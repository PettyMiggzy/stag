04 · Collectibles

The Hooded 20 (NFTs)#

The Hooded 20 is a collection of exactly twenty unique, 1-of-1 fully-animated NFTs. There are no duplicates, ever — once a stag is minted, that stag is gone. Every one of the twenty belongs to one of five rarity tiers:

Common

The most plentiful tier and the entry point into the collection.

Rare

A step up — scarcer and more coveted around the fires.

Epic

Uncommon animated legends with real presence.

Legendary

Very few exist. A trophy of Sherwood.

Mythic

The rarest stags of all — the crown of the Hooded 20.

Two ways to mint

  • Pick. Choose a specific available stag from the grid and mint exactly that one, paying its tier price. You know precisely what you're getting.
  • Surprise Draw. A rarity-weighted on-chain gamble — cheaper than picking, but the top tiers are rare to hit. You still can never receive a duplicate; the draw only ever lands on a stag that hasn't been minted.
💰

Prices are set on-chain per tier (the contract's tierPrice) and shown live on the mint page. Because they can change on-chain, this doc doesn't quote a fixed ETH price — always read the live price on /nfts before confirming.

Royalties & whitelist

  • Secondary royalty: a 5% (500 bps) royalty on secondary sales routes to the RevenueSplitter — which feeds the staking reward pool (see Revenue Flow).
  • Free-mint whitelist: whitelisted wallets mint at 0 — the contract charges them no tier price.
05 · Real Yield

Staking (real ETH yield)#

Staking lets you lock $STAG — or any whitelisted token — for a fixed term and earn real Robinhood ETH streamed from the reward pool. This is not inflationary emissions; it is actual ETH funded by mint revenue and royalties.

Lock tiers & multipliers

30 days

1× reward multiplier — the flexible tier.

60 days

1.5× reward multiplier — a middle commitment.

90 days

2× reward multiplier — the deepest lock, the biggest boost.

On top of the lock multiplier, a holding multiplier stacks: the larger your position, the higher the multiplier applied. Longer locks and bigger holdings compound together.

Rewards, maturity & the early-unstake penalty

  • Rewards accrue during the lock but only unlock at maturity. Let the term run and you claim the full amount in ETH.
  • Early unstake = 15% penalty on your principal, sent to the penalty recipient wallet — plus you forfeit all pending rewards. Unstaking before maturity is deliberately costly.
⚠️

Don't unstake early unless you mean it. Before maturity you lose 15% of your stake and give up every reward accrued so far. Rewards are only yours once the lock matures.

NFT staking (lock in place)

You can stake your Hooded 20 to boost your position. The NFT is locked in place — it never leaves your wallet; the staking contract simply marks it as locked. Unstake to release it. You keep custody the whole time.

Withdraw split (optional)

When you unstake, you may optionally split the withdrawn tokens across up to 3 wallets by share %. It applies to whichever token you withdraw; any remainder returns to you. Only your own staking wallet can set or change the split.

⛲

Where does the pool's ETH come from? Mint revenue and royalties, split 90/10 into the reward pool — plus anyone can donate ETH directly. See Revenue Flow.

06 · No Custody

Sherwood Pact (wallet-free, proof-of-hold)#

The Sherwood Pact is for holders who don't want to connect and lock tokens in a staking contract. You keep your $STAG in your own wallet the entire time — no custody, no transfer — and earn by proving you held.

How a pact works

  • Pay a small entry fee (~0.01 ETH, set on-chain) and pledge to hold ≥ X $STAG for a chosen window without moving it.
  • Your tokens never leave your wallet. There is nothing to withdraw because nothing was ever deposited.
  • Held the whole window → claim your refund (~0.005 ETH, set on-chain) plus a reward.
  • Didn't hold → your entry is forfeited (it covers backend / oracle costs).

Why proof-of-hold, and how it's verified

A smart contract cannot read your past balance — it only sees the present. So continuous holding is checked off-chain by the bubble-map indexer (the same engine behind the Stag Hood Maps), which has the full transfer history. A dedicated oracle key then resolves the pact on-chain based on that verdict.

🛟

Safety net — your funds are never trapped. If the oracle never resolves your pact, you can reclaim your full entry after the window plus a grace period. One open pact per wallet.

07 · Proof You Can't Rug

Stag Locker (LP & token lock)#

The Stag Locker at /locker is a permissionless, non-custodial time-lock for liquidity and team tokens on Robinhood Chain. Any project can lock its LP or supply, get a public proof link, and show its community it can't rug — the tokens are frozen on-chain until the unlock date, and no one — not even the locker admin — can pull them out early.

What you can lock

  • Any Robinhood Chain ERC-20 — your project token, team allocation, treasury, or a partner token.
  • Uniswap V3 LP positions — lock the liquidity NFT itself so the pool can't be pulled.

Two release types

Cliff

Everything stays locked until the unlock date, then unlocks 100% at once. The classic "LP locked until [date]."

Linear vesting

Unlocks continuously from start to end. Claim the vested portion any time as it releases — great for team tokens that drip out over months.

Pick a preset term — 60 days, 90 days, 180 days, 1 year, or 3 years — or set a custom unlock time.

Cost: burn $STAG, or hold $STAG and lock free

  • Burn model: locking burns 2,000,000 $STAG per 30 days of lock — so a 90-day lock burns ~6M and a 1-year lock ~24M — capped at 24M (a multi-year lock never costs more than a year's worth). Burned to the dead address: the longer people lock, the more $STAG is permanently removed from supply.
  • Hold & lock free: wallets holding 10,000,000+ $STAG lock for FREE — no burn at all. So the cost is really "hold $STAG or burn $STAG" — either way it drives $STAG demand.
🔥

The exact burn for your chosen duration is previewed live on /locker before you confirm. Hold 10M $STAG and it simply shows FREE.

You can only strengthen a lock — never weaken it

  • Extend the unlock date further out, or top up more tokens into an existing lock.
  • Transfer ownership of a lock to a new wallet (e.g. hand LP control to a multisig).
  • Collect V3 trading fees while your liquidity stays locked — fees go to the lock owner, principal never moves.
  • There is no early-unlock, no shorten, no admin override. Withdraw is only possible after the unlock date (or the vested portion, for vesting).

Why it's trustworthy

  • Non-custodial & admin-proof. The admin can only tune the fee/burn settings — the contract has no function that lets anyone move locked assets. Verified by audit.
  • Publicly verifiable. Every lock has an on-chain ID; anyone can look it up on the Verify tab and share a proof link with the community.
  • Audited & simulated. Multiple independent security-audit passes to a clean final verdict, plus hundreds of randomized invariant simulations (solvency, custody, no-early-release) before launch.
🔒

Locking is irreversible until the unlock date. Double-check the token, amount, release type, and duration before you confirm — there is no early exit by design. That's the whole point.

Two honest caveats (read before trusting a lock)

  • Vesting locks release continuously. A linear-vesting lock is not "fully locked until the end" — the vested portion becomes claimable as it unlocks, so its truly-locked amount decreases over time by design. For an "all locked until date X" guarantee, use a Cliff lock. The verify page labels which one a lock is.
  • An LP-NFT lock ≠ proof of locked value. A locked Uniswap V3 position can hold any (or zero) liquidity. Confirming a tokenId is locked here proves the NFT can't move — always check the position's actual liquidity on-chain before trusting a "liquidity locked" claim.

09 · Explorer

Stag Hood Maps (bubble-map explorer)#

The Stag Hood Maps at maps.stagwifhood.fun are a whole-chain holder-relationship explorer. Paste any Robinhood Chain token address and it draws the network of who holds it and how they're connected.

How to read the map

  • Nodes = holders. A bubble's size is its share of the token supply — bigger bubble, bigger holder.
  • Edges = real token transfers between holders.
  • Clusters group wallets that are related. The two signals: wallets that transacted in the same block, and — the strongest signal — wallets funded by the same source wallet ("funded together"), a classic sybil / insider pattern.
  • Isolated bubbles are simply wallets with no detected connections — not suspicious, just unconnected.

10 · Assurance

Security & Audits#

All four launch contracts — HoodedTwenty, StagStaking, RevenueSplitter, and SherwoodPact — underwent multiple independent security-audit passes plus hundreds of randomized invariant simulations before the mainnet deploy.

The Stag Locker was audited separately and even harder, because it holds third-party funds: four independent adversarial audit passes (custody/rug-vectors, vesting & arithmetic, reentrancy & hostile tokens, and contract-vs-UI consistency), a 49-test suite including dedicated attack tests, and 900+ randomized invariant simulations asserting solvency, custody, and no-early-release every step. Confirmed: the admin can never move locked assets, vesting can never over-release, and no reentrancy or solvency path exists.

After deployment, the on-chain wiring was verified to match the intended design:

  • Splitter → pool routing confirmed at 90 / 10.
  • The NFT locker points at the StagStaking contract.
  • The NFT royalty recipient is the RevenueSplitter.
  • Ownership, oracle, and penalty-recipient roles are all set to the intended admin wallet.
🛡️

Audits and simulations reduce risk; they don't eliminate it. Smart contracts are experimental software — interact within your risk tolerance and verify addresses against the Contracts table.
