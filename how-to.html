Guide 01

Set up your wallet & add Robinhood Chain#

You need an EVM wallet and the Robinhood Chain network configured. This takes a couple of minutes.

  1. Install a wallet
    Install MetaMask (browser extension or mobile app) from its official site, and create or import a wallet. Keep your seed phrase offline and never share it.
  2. Add the Robinhood Chain network
    Add a custom network with these exact parameters:
    • Network Name: Robinhood Chain
    • Chain ID: 4663 (hex 0x1237)
    • RPC URL: https://rpc.mainnet.chain.robinhood.com
    • Currency Symbol: ETH
    • Block Explorer: https://robinhoodchain.blockscout.com
  3. Or let the site do it — one tap
    Easiest: open any app page (/nfts, /stake, /pact) and tap the ＋ Add RH Chain button in the wallet bar — it adds Robinhood Chain to your wallet in one prompt. Then hit Connect Wallet. On iPhone, open the page inside your wallet's own browser (SafePal → Browser, or MetaMask → Browser) first — Safari can't see wallets.
  4. If mint/stake buttons seem dead
    99% of the time it's the network. Make sure your wallet is on Robinhood Chain (4663) — tap ＋ Add RH Chain, approve it, switch to it, then reconnect. Custom chains never come pre-installed, so this one-time add is always needed.
💡

All the network values above also live in the Docs → Network & Token with copy buttons.

Guide 02

Get ETH & buy $STAG#

Every action on Robinhood Chain costs a little ETH for gas. Get some Robinhood-Chain ETH into your wallet first, then buy $STAG.

  1. Fund your wallet with Robinhood-Chain ETH
    Make sure your connected wallet holds some ETH on chain 4663. This ETH pays gas for minting, staking, and pacts — without it, transactions can't go through.
  2. Buy $STAG
    Head to the buy link and swap for $STAG:
    • stagwifhood.fun/bridge
    Confirm the swap in your wallet. When it lands, $STAG shows up in your balance.
  3. (Optional) Add the token to your wallet
    To see your balance in MetaMask, import the $STAG token address 0xcC142366735c882F7885d3c747db99e45E13E453 (18 decimals).
Guide 03

Mint a Hooded 20#

Claim one of the twenty 1-of-1 animated stags. No duplicates, ever.

  1. Go to the mint page & connect
    Open /nfts and hit Connect Wallet. Approve the Robinhood Chain network if prompted.
  2. Choose Pick or Surprise Draw
    • Pick — scroll the grid, choose a specific available stag, and mint it at its tier price. You get exactly that stag.
    • Surprise Draw — a cheaper rarity-weighted gamble; you can't pick which stag, and the top tiers are rare to hit, but you'll never get a duplicate.
  3. Confirm in your wallet
    Approve the transaction. Prices are set on-chain and shown live on the page — always mint at the price displayed there.
🎟️

Whitelisted wallets mint free — the contract charges them 0. If you're on the whitelist, your price shows as 0.

Guide 04

Stake $STAG for ETH rewards#

Lock $STAG for a fixed term and earn real Robinhood ETH from the reward pool.

  1. Go to the stake page & connect
    Open /stake and hit Connect Wallet.
  2. Pick a lock tier
    Choose your term — longer locks earn a bigger reward multiplier:
    • 30 days → 1×
    • 60 days → 1.5×
    • 90 days → 2×
    A holding multiplier stacks on top — bigger positions earn more.
  3. Enter an amount & approve
    Type an amount (or hit MAX), then approve the token spend in your wallet — a one-time permission per token.
  4. Stake
    Hit Stake and confirm. Your position and multiplier appear under "Your Position."
  5. Claim ETH & Unstake
    Rewards accrue during the lock and unlock at maturity — use Claim ETH to collect. Use Unstake after maturity to withdraw your principal.
⚠️

Early unstake is costly. Unstaking before your term matures charges a 15% penalty on your principal and forfeits all pending rewards. Only unstake early if you truly need to.

Guide 05

Stake your Hooded 20 NFTs#

Boost your staking position with your NFTs — locked in place, never transferred.

  1. Open the NFT staking section
    On /stake (connected), scroll to "Stake Your Hooded 20." The stags you own appear here.
  2. Stake NFT (lock in place)
    Hit Stake NFT on any stag you own. It's locked to boost your position but stays in your wallet the whole time — no transfer, no custody.
  3. Unstake to release
    Hit Unstake on a staked NFT to release the lock whenever you like.

Guide 06

Seal a Sherwood Pact (no wallet lock)#

Earn by simply holding — your $STAG never leaves your wallet.

  1. Go to the pact page & connect
    Open /pact and hit Connect Wallet.
  2. Set your hold amount & window
    Enter how much $STAG you pledge to hold and for how many days (the page shows the allowed range). You're promising to hold at least that amount, untouched, for the whole window.
  3. Pay the entry fee
    Hit Seal Pact and pay the entry (~0.01 ETH, set on-chain). Your tokens are not deposited — nothing leaves your wallet.
  4. Hold the whole time, then claim
    Keep holding without moving your $STAG. Held the full window → Claim your refund (~0.005 ETH) + reward. Didn't hold → the entry is forfeited.
  5. Safety net: reclaim if unresolved
    If the oracle never resolves your pact, you can reclaim your full entry after the window + a grace period — your funds are never trapped. One open pact per wallet.

Guide 08

Lock LP or team tokens (Stag Locker)#

Prove to your community you can't rug. The Stag Locker freezes your liquidity or team tokens on-chain until a date you choose — non-custodial, and no one can pull them early. This guide is for project teams and LPs.

🟢

Live on Robinhood Chain: 0x6F69fEbE30ba7348901a523bcc9a6fAA5b493160 — view on Blockscout ↗

  1. Go to the locker & connect
    Open /locker and hit Connect Wallet on Robinhood Chain (chainId 4663).
  2. Pick what to lock
    Choose an ERC-20 token (paste its address and an amount) or a Uniswap V3 LP position (its liquidity NFT). Any Robinhood Chain token works.
  3. Choose release type & duration
    • Cliff — stays 100% locked until the unlock date, then fully unlocks.
    • Linear vesting — releases continuously; claim the vested portion any time.
    Then pick a term: 60, 90, 180 days, 1 year, or 3 years.
  4. Cover the cost (burn or free)
    Locking burns 2,000,000 $STAG per 30 days of lock (so ~6M for 90 days, ~24M for a year, capped at 24M) — the exact amount is previewed live before you confirm. Or, if you hold 10,000,000+ $STAG, it shows FREE and burns nothing. Approve the $STAG spend if a burn applies.
  5. Approve the asset & Lock
    Approve the token/LP for the locker (one-time per asset), then hit Lock and confirm. You get an on-chain lock ID.
  6. Share your proof
    Open the Verify tab, look up your lock ID, and share the proof link so anyone can confirm your LP/tokens are locked until the date shown.
  7. Manage it (strengthen only)
    Any time you can extend the date, top up more tokens, transfer ownership, or collect V3 trading fees while liquidity stays locked. You can never shorten or early-exit a lock.
🔒

Locks are final until the unlock date. Confirm the token, amount, release type, and duration before you sign — there's no early exit by design. Cliff = all locked until the date; Vesting = releases continuously (claimable as it vests). For LP, remember a locked V3 NFT can hold any amount of liquidity — verify the real amount. See the Docs → Stag Locker for the full mechanics.

Guide 09

Explore the Stag Hood Maps#

Visualize who holds any Robinhood Chain token and how the wallets connect — free, no wallet needed.

  1. Open the maps
    Go to maps.stagwifhood.fun. No connection or gas required — it's served from an indexed database and loads instantly.
  2. Paste a token address
    Enter any Robinhood Chain token address (try $STAG: 0xCDdB…9F49). Every ERC-20 on the chain is auto-discovered.
  3. Read the bubbles & clusters
    Bubble size = share of supply. Edges = transfers. Clusters = wallets in the same block or funded by the same source. Isolated bubbles are just unconnected wallets. Funding links are a signal, not proof — see the Docs methodology.

Troubleshooting#

"Wrong price" on mint

Refresh the page — the tier price may have changed on-chain. Whitelisted wallets pay 0.

"Wrong network"

Switch your wallet to Robinhood Chain (chainId 4663). The site can prompt the switch when you connect.

Transaction stuck pending

Make sure you hold Robinhood-Chain ETH for gas, then retry. No RH-ETH = no confirmations.

Balances not showing

Disconnect and reconnect your wallet. Confirm you're on chain 4663 and that the token is imported.
