00What $STAGWIFHOOD actually is

The story: Robin Hood left Sherwood for Wall Street — but forgot his hood. A rogue stag put it on and never gave it back. That stag is $STAG, the face of Robinhood Chain.

Strip the lore and here's the real thing: $STAGWIFHOOD is a full Web3 product suite built on Robinhood Chain. It's not just a token you buy and hope. It's a token wired into a whole machine — NFTs you can mint, staking that pays you real ETH, a treasury that locks itself, games you can play, and a prize system that pays holders as the market cap grows.

The one-line pitch: "The stag steals from the chart and gives to the holders." Every utility below is a way that promise is kept — and every one is already deployed and verifiable on-chain. We say "live," we mean live.

03The Hooded 20

Two ways to mint

  • Pick — you choose the exact stag you want. Price scales with its rarity tier: the rarer the stag, the more it costs.

Every ID has real multi-trait metadata — actual attributes, not generative filler. There are only 20, ever.

A Hooded 20 isn't just art — it's a yield multiplier. Holding one boosts your staking rewards, and each NFT can be staked in place (see Stag Staking) to earn ETH without leaving your wallet. Holders also get whitelist priority in every Prize Heist.

05Stag Staking — the core earner

This is the big one. Stake and earn real ETH, streamed to everyone who's staked, split by how much "weight" they have. Claim anytime.

What you can stake

  • $STAG tokens — the classic way.
  • Hooded 20 NFTs — and here's the magic: it's lock-in-place. Your NFT never leaves your wallet. The contract just marks it locked. You keep custody the whole time.
  • Both at once — for the biggest weight.

How your rewards are sized ("weight")

Your slice of the ETH stream is your weight ÷ everyone's weight. Weight is:

tokens × lock-tier × holding-time × NFT multiplier

  • Lock tier — you pick a lock length; longer locks give a bigger multiplier.
  • Holding time — the longer you've held, the more you're boosted.
  • NFT multiplier — holding Hooded 20 NFTs multiplies your token stake on top.
Where the ETH comes from (say this confidently)

90% of every Hooded 20 mint flows straight into the staking reward pool via an on-chain splitter — no human deciding when to pay. The stream refills itself as people mint. It's real revenue, not printed tokens.

The catch — early unstake

  • Unstake tokens before your lock ends → 15% penalty + you forfeit pending rewards.
  • Unstake an NFT early → you just forfeit pending rewards (no penalty, you keep the NFT).
🎁 The payout

Real ETH, streaming right now — claim whenever you want. This is the headline utility; make sure every holder knows it exists.

06Sherwood Pact — "non-wallet staking"

The Pact solves the one objection every staker has: "I don't want to hand my tokens to a contract." With the Pact, you never do.

The Pact is proof-of-hold. You keep your $STAG in your own wallet the entire time. Nothing is deposited, nothing is locked, no contract holds your bag. You commit to holding through a window; an indexer verifies that you actually did; and the payout settles on-chain. That's it.

How to explain the difference

Staking = your tokens go into the contract to earn. The Pact = your tokens stay in your wallet, the chain just confirms you held, and you get paid for it. Same reward-for-conviction idea, zero custody, no lockup, exit anytime.

🎁 The payout

A diamond-hands reward for holding — without ever giving up control of your bag. Perfect for holders who refuse to deposit into anything.

10How the ETH actually moves

Commit this to memory — it's the whole economic story in four beats:

  1. Someone mints a Hooded 20 or a Saint (pays ETH).
  2. The splitter instantly forks that ETH (90/10 or 60/30/10).
  3. The biggest slice lands in the reward pool (plus burns + a little team).
  4. The pool streams ETH to stakers, who claim anytime. More mints → more rewards. It's a loop.

14Talk track / FAQ — answer any holder

"What do I get for holding?"

Three things: stake for real ETH, win physical prizes at every market-cap milestone, and your NFTs multiply your rewards. It's not hold-and-hope — it pays.

"Is the staking yield just more of your token?"

No — it's real ETH, funded by 90% of every mint. No inflation, no printing. You can watch the pool on-chain.

"I don't want to lock my tokens in a contract."

Then use the Sherwood Pact — you keep your $STAG in your own wallet the whole time and still get paid for holding. Nothing is deposited.

"If I stake my NFT, do you take it?"

No. NFT staking is lock-in-place — it never leaves your wallet. The contract just marks it locked while it earns.

"Can I stake my Saints for ETH right now?"

The Vault is live and Saints are registered, but it isn't funded yet, so rewards aren't flowing there yet. Token + Hooded 20 staking IS paying ETH right now. (Don't overpromise the Vault.)

"My wallet won't connect on mobile."

Use an EVM wallet — MetaMask, Trust, Rainbow. Phantom and Jupiter are Solana wallets and can't connect. On mobile, tap Connect → pick MetaMask in the WalletConnect popup, or open the site inside MetaMask's own browser.

"How do I know you won't rug?"

Liquidity and tokens are locked in the Stag Locker with public on-chain proof, every contract is verified on Blockscout, and the money routing is automated — no treasury middleman. Verify it yourself.
