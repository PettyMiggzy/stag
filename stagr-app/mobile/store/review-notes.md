# Notes for the App Review team (paste into App Store Connect and Play Console)

STAGR is a business tool for small service companies (quotes, jobs, invoices, customer texts). It needs an account.

- Password: (and its password)

Notes:
- Sign-up is free and happens in the app. Account deletion is in Settings > "Delete my account" (deletes the person's login,
  or the whole business if they are the owner).
- Notifications are used for new customer requests, approved quotes and paid invoices.
- Customers of the business receive texts only if they agreed to receive texts. The business sets this up in Settings.
- No payment is taken in the app. Invoices show the business's own payment instructions.
