# Privacy answers (App Store "App Privacy" and Google Play "Data safety")

Collected and linked to the person's account, used only to run the app (not for ads, not sold, not shared for marketing):
- Name, email address, phone number (account and the business's staff)
- Customer contact details and addresses the business enters about its own customers
- Photos the business attaches to jobs
- Messages sent to customers through the app (SMS provider: Twilio)
- Push notification token (to send notifications)
- Diagnostics: basic server logs (no tracking SDKs, no advertising ID)

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). The owner can delete the whole business, and any person can delete their own
account, inside the app. Data is deleted immediately.
No tracking across other companies' apps or websites. No health, financial-account, location, contacts or browsing data.
