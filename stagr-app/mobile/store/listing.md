**Description**

STAGR is the field service app for small crews: junk removal, cleaning, lawn care, handyman, pressure washing and
more. Everything you need to run the day, in one place on your phone.

- Customers and properties, with every job and message in one history
- Requests from your online booking page land in your inbox
- Build and send quotes. Customers approve with one tap, no app needed
- Schedule jobs and visits, assign your crew, and use checklists and photos on site
- Invoice from the job and get paid. Unpaid invoices get friendly reminders
- Text customers: appointment reminders, quote follow-ups and review requests
- Reports on money collected, what is overdue and which customers come back
- Team logins for owners, admins and workers

Your data belongs to your business. Delete your account any time inside the app (Settings).
