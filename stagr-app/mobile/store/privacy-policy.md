# STAGR privacy policy (DRAFT: review it, add your company name and support email, then publish it on a page you control)

Last updated: (date)

STAGR ("we") provides a field service app for small businesses. This policy explains what we collect and why.

**Information we collect.** Account details (name, email, phone, password stored as a salted hash); information a business
enters about its own customers (names, phone numbers, emails, addresses, job and invoice details); photos a business attaches to
jobs; messages sent to customers through STAGR; and a push notification token if you allow notifications.

**How we use it.** To run the app for the business that entered it: show it to that business's team, send the quotes,
invoices and text messages the business asks us to send, and send notifications. We do not sell personal information and we do
not use it for advertising or tracking.

**Who sees it.** Only the business and its team. Our service providers process it for us: hosting (Vercel), database (Neon),
and text messaging (Twilio). We share data otherwise only if the law requires it.

**Text messages.** A business may only text customers who agreed to receive texts. Customers can reply STOP at any time.

**Your choices.** You can change or delete information in the app. You can delete your account in Settings > "Delete my account".
An owner deleting their account deletes the whole business and its data immediately. To ask us anything, email (support email).

**Security.** Data is sent over HTTPS and passwords are hashed. No system is perfectly secure.

**Children.** STAGR is for businesses and is not directed to children under 13.

**Changes.** We will post changes on this page.
