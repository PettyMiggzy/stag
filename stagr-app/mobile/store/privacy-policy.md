# STAGR privacy policy

The published policy is the web page, not this file:

- **Live URL (use this in both stores):** https://stagr.junkjunkiesindiana.com/privacy.html
- **Source file:** `public/privacy.html`
- **Account deletion page:** https://stagr.junkjunkiesindiana.com/delete-account.html (`public/delete-account.html`)
- **Support page:** https://stagr.junkjunkiesindiana.com/support.html (`public/support.html`)

Publisher: Junk Junkies. Contact: info@junkjunkiesindiana.com.

## Summary of what the policy says (keep in step with the page)

- **Collected:** account name, email, phone and a salted scrypt password hash; business profile; the business's customer
  records (names, phones, emails, addresses, notes); requests with photos; quotes; jobs and visits with photos; invoices
  and payment records (amount, method, date, never card numbers); text and email history; time-clock entries; expenses;
  a sign-in cookie; IP address for rate limiting; a push notification token only when notifications are enabled.
- **Not collected:** location, contacts, advertising ID. No ads, no analytics SDKs, no selling of data.
- **Service providers:** Neon (Postgres database), Vercel (hosting), Twilio (texts), Resend (email), Square (card
  checkout on Square's page under the business's own Square account), Firebase Cloud Messaging (push, when enabled),
  Google Fonts (font files).
- **Deletion:** in the app (More or the Me tab > My account > Delete my account, enter password and type DELETE), or by
  emailing info@junkjunkiesindiana.com from the account's email address (done within 30 days). Owner deletion removes
  the whole business; a team member's deletion removes only their login.
- **Retention:** kept while the account exists; removed from the live database at deletion; database backups expire
  within about 30 days.
- **Children:** not directed to children under 13.

Each business using STAGR also has its own customer-facing privacy policy and text-message terms, generated by the app at
`/privacy/<business-slug>` and `/sms-terms/<business-slug>`. Those are for the business's customers and carriers, not
for the app stores.

## Check before submitting

- Confirm Vercel is the host of stagr.junkjunkiesindiana.com. If it is hosted somewhere else, change the name in `public/privacy.html`.
- Confirm the backup retention in the Neon project settings is 30 days or less. If it is longer, change "30 days" on both pages.
- Open all three URLs on a phone, signed out, after deploying.
