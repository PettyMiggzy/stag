Delete your STAGR account

STAGR app, published by Junk Junkies

You can delete your STAGR account and its data at any time. The fastest way is inside the app. If you cannot sign in, you can ask us by email.

Delete it in the app

  1. Sign in to the STAGR app, or at stagr.junkjunkiesindiana.com/app.
  2. Open More. If you have a worker login, open the Me tab instead.
  3. Choose My account.
  4. Choose Delete my account.
  5. Enter your password, type DELETE, and confirm.

Deletion happens straight away and cannot be undone.

Cannot sign in?

First try Forgot your password? on the sign-in screen. If that does not work, email info@junkjunkiesindiana.com from the email address on the account and ask us to delete it. Tell us whether you want only your login removed or, if you are the owner, the whole business.

We may reply to confirm it is really you. We complete requests within 30 days.

What gets deleted

If you are the business owner

Deleting the owner account deletes the whole business:

  • The business profile, services and settings
  • All customers and their addresses and notes
  • All requests, quotes, jobs, visits and their photos
  • All invoices and payment records
  • All text and email message history
  • Time-clock entries, expenses and activity history
  • Every team member's login
  • The saved Square connection and any notification device tokens

The public booking page and any quote or invoice links sent to customers stop working. Your Square account itself is separate and is not touched.

If you are a team member (admin or worker)

Deleting your account removes your login: your name, email address, phone number, password hash and notification device tokens.

The business and its records (customers, jobs, invoices, and work entries such as job notes, photos and time-clock entries) stay with the business owner. To have those removed, ask the owner of the business.

What may be kept, and for how long

  • Data is removed from our live database immediately.
  • Copies in our database provider's backups expire on their own, normally within 30 days.
  • Texts and emails already delivered stay on the customers' phones and inboxes, and our texting, email and payment providers keep their own records as their policies and the law require.

Delete some data without deleting the account

You can remove individual customers, requests, quotes, jobs, invoices and photos inside the app at any time. If you need help, email info@junkjunkiesindiana.com.
