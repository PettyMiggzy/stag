Privacy Policy

STAGR app · Last updated November 11, 2026

Who we are

STAGR is a field-service app for small businesses: customers, requests, quotes, jobs, invoices, texting and team logins. It is published by Junk Junkies. It runs at stagr.junkjunkiesindiana.com and in the STAGR iPhone and Android apps. Questions: info@junkjunkiesindiana.com.

This policy covers people who sign in to STAGR (business owners and their team). If you are a customer of a business that uses STAGR, that business decides how your details are used. See "Customers of a business" below.

What we collect

Your account

  • Your name, email address and phone number.
  • Your password, stored only as a salted scrypt hash. We cannot read your password.
  • Your role in the business (owner, admin or worker).

Your business

  • Business profile: name, phone, email, city, state, time zone, tax rate, services and prices, and settings.
  • Customer records the business enters: names, company names, phone numbers, email addresses, service addresses, notes, tags and texting or email consent.
  • Requests (including ones sent through the business's online booking page), with any photos attached.
  • Quotes, jobs and visits, with checklists, notes and photos.
  • Invoices and payment records: amount, method and date. We never receive or store card numbers.
  • Message history: the texts and emails sent to customers through STAGR and their delivery status.
  • Time-clock entries, expenses and an activity log of what was done on each customer.

Technical information

  • A sign-in cookie (stagr_session) that keeps you signed in for up to 14 days. It is not used for tracking.
  • Your IP address, used to limit repeated sign-in, sign-up and password-reset attempts. Our hosting provider also keeps standard server logs.
  • A push notification device token (through Firebase Cloud Messaging), only if notifications are switched on in the app and you allow them. It is removed when you sign out or delete your account.
  • Photos: the app asks for the camera or photo library only when you choose to add a photo. Only the photos you pick are uploaded.

STAGR has no ads, no analytics or tracking tools, and no advertising ID. We do not collect your location, contacts or browsing history.

How we use it

Only to run the app for the business that entered the information: show it to that business's team, send the quotes, invoices, texts and emails the business asks us to send, send password-reset emails, send notifications, and keep accounts secure.

We do not sell personal information. We do not use it for advertising, and we do not share it with anyone for their marketing.

Who can see it

Only the business and its team. Each business's data is kept separate from every other business. Workers see a limited view chosen by their role.

These service providers process data for us, only as needed to provide their service:

  • Neon: the Postgres database where STAGR data is stored.
  • Our hosting provider: a cloud server in the United States that runs the app and website.
  • Twilio: sends text messages, when texting is set up. It receives the customer's phone number and the message.
  • Resend: sends email, when email is set up. It receives the email address and the message.
  • Square: card payments. A business can connect its own Square account. Customers then pay on Square's own checkout page, under that business's Square account. Square handles the card details; STAGR only records the amount, date and that it was paid.
  • Google Firebase Cloud Messaging: delivers push notifications, when they are switched on. It receives the device token and the notification text.
  • Google Fonts: the app loads its fonts from Google, which sees your IP address when it does.

We otherwise share information only if the law requires it.

Customers of a business

If a business that uses STAGR has your details, that business is responsible for them. Contact the business to see, correct or delete your information. Each business has its own privacy policy and text-message terms linked from its booking page. To stop text messages, reply STOP. You can also email us and we will pass your request to the business.

How long we keep it

  • We keep your data for as long as your account exists.
  • When you delete your account, it is removed from our live database straight away (see below).
  • Copies in our database provider's backups expire on their own, normally within 30 days.
  • Password-reset links expire after 1 hour.

Deleting your account and data

  • In the app: sign in, open More (or the Me tab), then My account, then Delete my account. Enter your password and type DELETE.
  • Cannot sign in? Use the account deletion page.
  • Owners: deleting the owner account deletes the whole business: customers, requests, quotes, jobs, invoices, photos, messages and every team login.
  • Team members: deleting your account removes your login and personal details. The business's records stay with the business.

You can also change or remove individual records inside the app at any time, or email us to ask for a copy of or a correction to your information.

Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Passwords are hashed. Saved Square connection keys are encrypted. Sign-in attempts are rate limited. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong password.

Children

STAGR is a tool for businesses. It is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child has given us information, email us and we will delete it.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will post the new version on this page and update the date at the top.
