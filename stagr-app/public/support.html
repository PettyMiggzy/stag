Common questions

I forgot my password

On the sign-in screen, tap Forgot your password? and enter your email. We send a link that works once and expires in 1 hour. Check your spam folder. If you are on a team, an owner or admin can also set a new password for you in Settings.

How do I add team members?

An owner or admin opens More, then Settings, and adds the person under Team with their name, email, a temporary password and a role. Workers get a simpler view with only the work assigned to them. Only the owner can add an admin.

How do I take card payments?

Open More, then Settings, and connect your own Square account. After that, your customers see a Pay by card button on the invoice and deposit links you send them. They pay on Square's secure checkout page and the money goes to your Square account. STAGR never sees card numbers. You can still record cash, check and other payments by hand.

Why are my texts or emails saved as "preview"?

Texting and email need to be set up before anything is sent. Until then STAGR saves each message as a preview so you can see what customers would get. Texting also requires carrier registration for your business. Email us and we will help you get set up.

How do I delete my account?

Open More (or the Me tab), then My account, then Delete my account. Full details, including what to do if you cannot sign in, are on the account deletion page.

How is my data handled?

See the privacy policy. No ads, no tracking, and we do not sell data.
