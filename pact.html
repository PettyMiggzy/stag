Seal a Sherwood Pact

Proof-of-hold, no custody. Pledge to hold ≥ X $STAG for a set window without moving it. Pay a small entry now — your tokens never leave your wallet. Here's exactly how it settles:

  • ✓Held the whole window → you get a partial refund of your entry + a reward on top. (Entry minus refund covers backend/indexer costs — it's not a full refund.)
  • ✕Moved or sold early → you forfeit the entry. No penalty on your tokens — you just lose the entry fee.
  • ⏳Never resolved → reclaim your full entry after the window + grace period. Always fully funded.
