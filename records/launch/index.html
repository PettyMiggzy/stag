Stag House Records · The Launchpad

Back the record.

Other launchpads launch coins. Ours launches music. Put your $STAG behind a Stag House record, grow your rack of antler points, and get your wallet on the wall as one of the people who backed it.

1 · Stake on a recordPick a record and stake $STAG on it. Your tokens sit in the contract under your name. Nobody else can move them, the label included.
2 · Grow your rackYou earn antler points for every token, every day it stays in. Take any tokens out of a record and your points on that record start over.
3 · Backers get the perksYour wallet goes on the record's backers board. The label uses the board to pick who gets first access and backer-only editions on its drops.

Staking here pays nothing: no yield, no rewards in tokens or cash, no fees. You get your full amount back whenever you ask, to the same wallet. Antler points have no cash value and cannot be traded. Perks are chosen by the label and can change. You need a wallet with $STAG and a little ETH on Robinhood Chain for network fees. This is not an investment product.
