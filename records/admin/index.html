Stag House admin

Anyone can open this page, but only the owner wallet can make changes. Everything you do here is a transaction you approve in your wallet.

Status

Connect your wallet to see the status.

Add a song or an album

Add as many songs, albums and artists as you like, with no new contract. Each item gets its own price in ETH, its own copy limit, its own artist wallet, and the label's cut. The label cut, the artist wallet and the copy limit are locked in when you add it, so the artist can trust their deal. You can change the ETH price later as the ETH price moves. New items start closed; open them when ready.

Open or close one item

Closing an item stops new sales of just that item. Nothing else changes, and you can open it again any time.

Change a price

Prices are in ETH, so update them if ETH moves a lot. Buyers are protected: if you change the price while someone is buying, their purchase fails and nothing is charged.

Lock the artwork

After an item is on sale, lock it so its title and cover can never be changed. This cannot be undone.

Songs and albums added here show up on the Songs page automatically. The cover is stored inside the item's own data, so there is nothing to upload to a server.
