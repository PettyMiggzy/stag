For artists

Why artists sign with the House

We handle the polish and the promotion. You keep the music.

Keep your masters

Your recordings and your songs stay yours. The label earns from the services it provides on a release, never from owning the music.

A full release kit

Cover art in the House look, a 15-second promo with the stag, the pre-roll intro, a release page and a Telegram listening party.

Fan-funded videos

Each drop pays for the next video, and the progress is public. Artists without a budget get a real shoot.

Plain-English splits

Who gets what is written down in one page before you sign: artist, video fund, label. No surprises after the drop.

Inside the House

Questions

Straight answers

Do artists really keep their masters?

Yes. The label earns from the services it provides on a release, and the split is written on one page before signing.

Are the NFTs an investment?

No. Editions are collectibles with fan perks. We do not promise returns, and nothing here is financial advice.

Which chain and which launchpad?

The plan is Robinhood Chain through the Pons family launchpad. The exact setup is still being confirmed.

What does the label take?

A small published share of each drop plus fees for optional services. The numbers go on this page before the first drop.

Who owns what I buy?

You own the collectible edition. The music rights stay with the artist, and each release page spells out what holders may do.
