Questions

Straight answers

What exactly am I buying?

A limited digital collectible NFT on Robinhood Chain: an edition of the Hood Rich album (100 copies) or of the WYTE BOYZ single (100 copies). You pay in ETH and the NFT lands in your own wallet.

Can I hear the full songs?

For now this site plays 20-second clips. Full-track listening for collectors is the next thing being built.

Are the NFTs an investment?

No. Editions are collectibles. We do not promise returns, and nothing here is financial advice.

Who owns what I buy?

You own the collectible edition. The music rights stay with the artist, and each release page spells out what holders may do.

Where does my payment go?

Straight into the on-chain contract, which pays the artist's payout wallet (and the label's share, where a release has one) automatically. You can read the contract on the block explorer.
