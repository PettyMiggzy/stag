Our story

Built by a real service company.

STAGR started inside Junk Junkies Texas, a junk removal company with four crews in Spring, Tomball, Cypress and College Station. We needed one place to answer every lead fast, send quotes customers can approve from their phone, schedule the crews and get paid. So we built our own.

Every feature here came from something our crews ran into on the job.
