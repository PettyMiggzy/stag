STAG

Claim your territory.

Four Texas junk removal crews wear the same stag. Spring, Tomball, Cypress and College Station each hold their own territory, and STAG is the name, the software and the community that ties them together.

The work

Built on real trucks.

These are working crews, not a mockup. Every job starts with a free quote from photos, and every price is based on how much room your stuff takes up in a 16-foot trailer.

Crew software

One list for every job.

The same software runs all four crews. It started as a CRM for one truck and grew from what the crews needed in the field and in the office.

  • Every request in one placeQuote requests from all four crew websites land in one list, with the customer's photos.
  • Quotes customers approve from a textItemized lines, tax and deposit. The customer taps Approve on their phone.
  • Invoices and payments in a tapRecord cash, check, Zelle or card, including partial payments.
  • Crew photos pin the mapBefore and after photos with GPS go to the right city page when a job is done.
  • A Today list that says who to call firstLeads waiting too long turn red, because the fastest reply wins the job.
