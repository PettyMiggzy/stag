The idea · draft

A token with trucks behind it.

Most tokens are a logo and a promise. STAGR is tied to four working businesses with real crews, real trailers and real customers in Texas. This is the draft concept for how the two connect.

01

Fees to the fleet

Proposed: a share of token trading fees goes to the crews' equipment, such as trucks, trailers and signage, so the token supports the work it is named after.

02

The marketplace

Junk Junkies is building a marketplace where customers set a ceiling price and haulers bid it down. STAGR is the community around it.

Open the marketplace preview ↗
03

Herd votes

Holders vote on community choices: which city a crew expands to next, truck names, and the crew of the month.

04

Upgrades

The idle territory game above is the start. Perks that can be paid for with STAGR are planned for later and are not for sale yet.
