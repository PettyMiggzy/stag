How it grows

Organic first. Slow cook.

The plan is to grow through real-world marketing instead of relying only on crypto promotion.

1

Real-world business content

Every day the founder already posts and promotes his real-world businesses on Instagram, YouTube, TikTok, Facebook and Reels. StagR CRM is built right into that content, so it gets marketed organically along with everything else.

2

Music through StagHouse Records

New music is released through StagHouse Records. Each music video includes a 15-second clip directing viewers to $STAG. Growth from this may be slow and steady, or faster if a song or video takes off.

3

A marketplace, later

The plan is to add a marketplace for selling music, starting with the label's own releases to get things moving. NFTs and other digital products may become part of it.

4

Tools for real businesses

The long-term goal is to tie $STAG into the founder's real-world business and explore tools that real-world businesses can actually use. Exactly what that looks like is still being worked out, including fees and the revenue structure.
